<idea>
  <design_movement>Zen Minimalist Physics</design_movement>
  <core_principles>
    1. Serenity and focus; the game is a meditative experience.
    2. Soft, organic shapes and motion.
    3. Muted, harmonious color palette.
    4. Unobtrusive UI that blends into the environment.
  </core_principles>
  <color_philosophy>
    Pastel tones on a soft off-white or cream background. Balls are soft shades of sage, coral, and slate blue. The goal is relaxation and satisfying physics.
  </color_philosophy>
  <layout_paradigm>
    Open canvas. The score is large but semi-transparent, placed in the background or very subtly at the top center, acting as part of the scenery rather than a dashboard.
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Soft Shadows**: Deep, diffuse shadows to give balls a tactile, floating feel.
    2. **Fluid Morphing**: Balls don't just pop; they stretch and squash slightly on impact.
    3. **Frosted Glass**: UI elements (if any) use a backdrop-filter blur.
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    Gentle and fluid. Splitting is like a bubble dividing; merging is like water droplets joining.
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    Eased, natural physics. Bounce effects are dampened slightly to feel heavy but bouncy.
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    Header: 'Playfair Display' for an elegant touch.
    Body/UI: 'Lato' or 'Nunito' for rounded, friendly readability.
  </typography_system>
</idea>
</text>

    Grid-breaking. The score might be a giant number on the side, rotated 90 degrees, or integrated into a geometric sidebar. The game container is a clearly defined canvas within the artistic composition.
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Geometric Primitives**: Balls are perfect circles with flat colors, no gradients.
    2. **Thick Strokes**: Heavy borders on UI elements.
    3. **Diagonal Lines**: Background patterns or UI dividers at 45-degree angles.
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    Mechanical and precise. Splitting is a sharp geometric dissection. Merging is a snap-fit.
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    Snappy, no overshoot. Movements are precise.
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    Header: 'Futura' or 'Spartan' (Geometric Sans).
    Body/UI: 'Space Grotesk' for a quirky, constructed feel.
